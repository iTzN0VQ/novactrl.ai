Electrical Diagnostics

A lying meter sends you to the wrong part. Measure under load. Trust the numbers.

Capacitor Under Load Test

Dual-clamp method

MFD = (Amps × 2652) ÷ Volts
Reading at ±6% of nameplate = healthy.
Anything outside that range — replace it.

Tests the capacitor under real load, not at rest, so it catches weak caps an ohmmeter won't flag. See the full write-up with worked example.

Ignitor Identification by Resistance

Hot surface ignitors

You can identify the ignitor type with your meter on ohms before you order the wrong part. Resistance reading tells you the voltage class.

ResistanceTypeVoltage
40 – 75 ΩSilicon Carbide120 VAC
10 – 30 ΩSilicon Nitride120 VAC
~ 40 ΩMini-igniter80 VAC
Field rule. Verify with the furnace board's spec — but the resistance reading gets you 90% of the way to the right part on the first truck stop.
