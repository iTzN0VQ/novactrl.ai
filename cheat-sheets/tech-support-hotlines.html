Before you dial. Tech support engineers respect a tech who shows up prepared. Have these four ready:
  1. Model & Serial. Off the nameplate, not memory.
  2. Error Code. From the board, not the homeowner.
  3. Pressures & Temps. Measured. Recent. Specific.
  4. One-sentence problem. "It's not working" doesn't count.
