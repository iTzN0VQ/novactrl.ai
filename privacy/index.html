Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use novactrl.ai (the “Site”), run by NovaCTRL (“NovaCTRL,” “we,” “us”). The short version: we don’t collect personal information through the Site.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The Site has no accounts, sign-ups, forms, cookies, ads, analytics or tracking pixels, and it loads nothing from other companies’ servers.

What stays on your device

The mobile tools remember your settings (your refrigerant, temperature unit, lookup direction and site elevation or barometer) in your browser’s local storage, so they carry from one tool to the next. Those settings stay on your device and are never sent to us. The readings you type into the tools aren’t saved or sent anywhere. You can erase the settings at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Hosting

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub logs technical information, such as visitors’ IP addresses, for security and to keep its service running. GitHub handles that information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don’t receive or use those logs.

Links to other sites

The tools’ Sources page links to refrigerant manufacturers and to NIST. When you follow a link, you leave the Site, and that site’s own privacy policy applies.

If you email us

If you write to support@novactrl.ai, we use your email address and message to reply to you, to keep a record of the conversation and to consider any ideas you send. We keep them only as long as we need them for those purposes.

Children

The Site is made for HVACR professionals and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we don’t collect personal information through the Site, there’s normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, email support@novactrl.ai and we’ll look into it and respond. We don’t sell or share personal information.

The Site doesn’t track you across websites, so Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals from your browser don’t change anything here.

Products and services still to come

EEV Telemetry and the NovaCTRL connector for Diamond System Intelligence aren’t available yet. If a future product or service needs personal information, for example to fill an order or to connect your Claude account, we’ll update this policy and explain what is collected and why before anything is collected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version here and update the effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@novactrl.ai.
