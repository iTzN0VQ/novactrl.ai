Superheat and subcooling

Superheat on the low side and subcooling on the high side, from your gauges and line temperatures.

Low side

High side

Superheat is suction line temperature minus the saturation temperature at suction pressure. On a blend that is the dew point, the temperature where the last drop of liquid boils off.

Subcooling is the saturation temperature at liquid line pressure minus liquid line temperature. On a blend that is the bubble point, the temperature where the first bubble of vapor forms.

Take the pressure and the temperature at the same spot, and charge to the manufacturer's numbers. On a heat pump in heating the coils swap jobs: the outdoor coil is the evaporator and the liquid line is at the indoor unit.
