Altitude and your gauges

What your gauges really read above sea level, and how far a sea-level chart is off.

A gauge reads pressure above the air around it. A PT chart printed in psig assumes sea level, 14.696 psia. Up high there is less air on top of the gauge, so the same reading means less absolute pressure and a colder saturation temperature. The chart reads high. Set your site at the top of the page and every number on it is corrected.

Site pressure from the standard atmosphere in the ASHRAE Handbook, Fundamentals, Chapter 1: p = 14.696 (1 − 6.8754×10⁻⁶ Z)5.2559 psia, Z in feet (the U.S. Standard Atmosphere, 1976). Weather apps report barometric pressure corrected to sea level. For the real number at your elevation use station pressure, or just enter the elevation.
