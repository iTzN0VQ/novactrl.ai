Gauge Diagnostic.

Enter pressures and temps. The tool computes superheat, subcool, condenser approach, and air ΔT, then matches your DP / SP / SH / SC pattern against the standard field troubleshooting chart. No AI guesses — just pattern match against published HVAC rules.

How it works. Maps suction + liquid line pressures through PT charts for your refrigerant to compute saturation temps. Superheat = suction line temp − evap saturation. Subcool = liquid line saturation − liquid line temp. Tags each of DP / SP / SH / SC as ↑ / ↓ / normal, then pattern-matches against the standard field troubleshooting chart (10 conditions including overcharge, undercharge, restriction, dirty condenser, low evaporator airflow, TXV bulb faults). Matching conditions are shown — collisions are surfaced as multiple candidates, not collapsed. Note: on most residential and light-commercial systems the "high side" service port reads liquid line pressure, not actual compressor discharge.
Source. Open-source on GitHub at github.com/iTzN0VQ/gauge-diagnostic — MIT licensed. Also ships a React + TypeScript hand-off pack for integrating into your own app.
