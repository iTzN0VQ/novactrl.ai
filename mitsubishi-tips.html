Smart Multi · Charging

Smart Multi — the right charging port

Three service ports on the unit, only one for charging. Charge by weight to the DSB number — not by superheat or subcool. Test run mode via DIP switches or the maintenance tool.

Ported Units · Maintenance

EEV maintenance on ported units

Pull the solenoid off. Scotch-Brite the valve body. Slide the solenoid back on. Twenty minutes of preventive maintenance prevents stuck-step faults in year three or four.
