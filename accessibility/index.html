Accessibility

Updated November 10, 2026

NovaCTRL wants novactrl.ai to work for every technician, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom or reduced motion. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Pages use plain, semantic HTML with headings, landmarks and a skip link.
  • Everything works from the keyboard, with a visible focus ring.
  • Text and controls are colored for contrast, on a white page that stays white even when your device is in dark mode.
  • The spray-paint logos have text alternatives, and the decorative paint drips are hidden from screen readers.
  • Motion turns off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Tool results are announced to screen readers when they change.

Known limits

Some results on the tools pages are also drawn as a chart; the same numbers are always shown in text next to it.

Tell us

If something on the Site doesn’t work for you, email support@novactrl.ai with the page and what happened, and we’ll work on a fix.
