VRF vs. VRV — what's the difference?

Same technology, different name. Here's the actual answer.

Variable Refrigerant Flow / Volume systems modulate refrigerant to multiple indoor units off one outdoor condenser. That's the technology. The naming is just trademark law.

VRVVRF
Daikin's trademark. They invented the technology in 1982 and own the name. If a spec sheet says "VRV," it's a Daikin product. The industry generic. Mitsubishi (City Multi), LG (Multi V), Samsung (DVM), Carrier — everyone else calls theirs VRF.

So when a GC tells you "we're going VRV in the new wing" — they mean Daikin, and you're going to need Daikin parts and Daikin commissioning. When they say "we're going VRF" — that's anyone else, and you ask which brand before you quote.

Why it matters. Inverter-driven VRF/VRV is what you'll be working on more and more — communicating systems with onboard logic, comm buses, and refrigerant-side precision a residential split never asked for. Get factory-trained on one platform and you'll never run out of work.
